About us

Our story, our standards, our method.

Hearth & Home Reviews was founded in 2024 by two remote workers — one in Brooklyn, one in Amsterdam — who were tired of furniture reviews that felt like rewritten press releases.

Why we started this site

We bought our first home-office chair based on a "Top 10 Best Office Chairs 2024" list. It cost $219, looked great in photos, and lasted eight months before the lumbar dial cracked and the gas cylinder started sinking every twenty minutes. When we tried to find an honest review, every top result read like a paid placement.

We don't want other remote workers, families, or first-time homeowners to make the same mistake. So we started testing furniture ourselves — at retail price, in real homes, for weeks at a time — and writing what we found.

Who writes the reviews

Every review on this site is written by one of two people:

  • Eliza Hartmann — co-founder, based in Amsterdam. Former product designer. Tests most home-office and bedroom categories. Has assembled over 40 pieces of flat-pack furniture in the last decade.
  • Marco Reyes — co-founder, based in Brooklyn. Former data analyst turned remote-work writer. Tests most living-room and dining-room categories. Lives with two rescue dogs who chew-test everything they can reach.

No content is produced by external contractors, AI alone, or manufacturers. We occasionally invite guest reviewers for specialty topics (e.g. outdoor furniture, vintage restoration), but always disclose when a piece is a guest contribution.

How we test

Every product is bought at full retail price by Hearth & Home Reviews. It ships to one of our two apartments (or, for furniture larger than a small kitchen table, is assembled by the delivery team).

Minimum test duration: 4 weeks. Most categories warrant 6–8 weeks. Beds and sofas are tested for a full eight weeks because those categories are the most expensive and the slowest to reveal problems.

Test methodology, by category:

  • Office chairs & desks: 8+ hours/day of remote work, at least 2 testers of different heights and weights.
  • Sofas: full-family use (kids, pets, spills), tested for noise, frame rigidity, and zipper durability.
  • Beds: slept on by two adults of different weights; measured slat integrity at 30 and 90 days.
  • Dining tables: 3 meals/day, dropped keys, juice-spill test, wobble test (loaded vs. unloaded).

What we won't do

Our editorial standards exist because we want to be trusted by readers — and because the moment a reader can't trust us, the site stops having a reason to exist.

  • We won't accept payment, gifts, or loaner products from manufacturers in exchange for review coverage.
  • We won't change a review's text or rating after publication to please a manufacturer.
  • We won't run sponsored posts disguised as editorial reviews.
  • We won't bury or omit the alternatives to our top pick — readers deserve a full picture.
  • We won't recommend a product we haven't personally tested.

How we're compensated

We earn revenue in three ways:

  1. Amazon Associates affiliate links — when you buy through our links, we earn a small commission.
  2. Newsletter sponsorship — three slots per year, clearly marked as "Sponsored".
  3. Reader donations — voluntary, via the newsletter signup page.

None of these influence which products earn which rankings. For the full disclosure, see our Affiliate Disclosure.
