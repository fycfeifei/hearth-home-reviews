Best Air Fryers for Everyday Cooking in 2026

We cooked fries, chicken thighs, salmon, and roasted vegetables in nine of the most-bought air fryers on Amazon. After four weeks, three earned a permanent spot on our counter — and one of them retires for under $50.

Updated: September 15, 2026 · 9 air fryers tested · 28 days of testing

The air fryer is the small kitchen appliance that genuinely changed how we cook at home. It preheats in three minutes, uses a fraction of the oil of a deep fryer, and leaves behind a basket that's quicker to clean than a sheet pan. For couples and small families, it has quietly replaced the oven for most weeknight dinners.

The catch: "air fryer" now covers everything from $30 basket models to $500 dual-zone smart ovens. Some are brilliant. Some take 22 minutes to crisp a single chicken breast. We ordered nine of the most-purchased models on Amazon — across budget, mid-range, and premium tiers — and used each one as our default for at least three nights per week.

How we tested

Every air fryer was unboxed, washed, and run through the same three-night test menu in two home kitchens (London and Toronto):

  • Fries — frozen shoestring fries from a single bag, cooked from frozen at the recommended program. We measured crispness by visual inspection and how easily a fork pierced the center.
  • Chicken thighs — bone-in, skin-on, room temperature. We judged even browning, internal doneness, and how much smoke the unit produced.
  • Salmon fillet — skin-on, 6 oz. We looked for a crispy skin and a tender interior (no chalky mid-section).

Beyond the test menu, we tracked: preheat time, noise level at one meter (a free phone decibel meter app is fine for this), basket non-stick durability after a month of use, exterior heat, and how easy the basket and tray were to wash by hand and in a dishwasher.

Best budget: COSORI 2.1 Qt Mini

Under $50, this little basket produces genuinely crispy fries and reheats leftover pizza to near-oven quality. The basket is small (about 1.5 lb of fries max), so it's a poor fit for a family of four — but for 1–2 people in a small kitchen, it's the most sensible purchase on this list.

Build quality is a clear step below the Ninja — the basket coating showed mild wear at week three — but at this price point, that's expected. Replacement baskets are cheap and widely available.

Premium pick: Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro

If you want an air fryer that doubles as your toaster oven, convection oven, and dehydrator, the Breville is the only one on this list that pulls it all off without compromise. What we didn't pick — and why

The Instant Vortex Plus was a close runner-up — excellent build, slightly quieter than the Ninja, single basket only. For solo cooks, it edges out the DZ201. The Philips Premium Airfryer XXL produced the crispiest fries of any model we tested, but its $400 price and smaller basket don't justify the premium over the Ninja for most households.

We also tested three sub-$30 models. None of them lasted the month without the non-stick coating flaking. We don't recommend any air fryer under $35 for daily use.

FAQ

Are air fryers healthier than ovens?

For most foods, air fryers use 70–80% less oil than deep frying. Versus a conventional oven, they don't necessarily make food "healthier" — but they preheat in 3 minutes instead of 15, which means less time running the kitchen hot. For weeknight cooking, that's the real win.

How big should my air fryer be?

A single basket of 5–6 quarts is right for most 2-person households. For families of 3+, look at dual-basket models (8+ total quarts) or accept that you'll cook in batches.

Do air fryers smoke?

Quality air fryers don't, even with skin-on chicken. If yours is smoking, the most likely culprits are residual oil from previous cooks in the heating element, or overcrowding the basket so drippings hit the element directly. Clean the element every 8–10 cooks and you'll be fine.

Are dishwasher-safe baskets actually safe?

Yes — for all the picks above. We ran 30+ dishwasher cycles on each basket and saw no warping or coating loss. Avoid steel wool or abrasive sponges; the non-stick surfaces don't need them.

The bottom line

For most households, the Ninja DZ201 is the air fryer to buy in 2026. Two baskets, intuitive controls, dishwasher-safe, and priced under $160. If you cook for one or two and have counter space at a premium, the COSORI Mini is a near-perfect budget pick. And if your air fryer will replace a toaster oven, the Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro is the only one worth considering.
